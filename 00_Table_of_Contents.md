# 🛡️ The Complete Non-Functional Testing Playbook

### A Comprehensive Guide: Performance, Security, Usability, Accessibility, and Every Other Way Software Fails Besides "The Feature Is Wrong"

---

> **Author**: Ghanendra Yadav
> **Target Audience**: QA professionals ready to move beyond functional testing
> **Total Curriculum**: 13 Parts, organized around ISO/IEC 25010
> **Key Feature**: Every real-world example is grounded in real portfolio project case
> studies — fintech, healthcare, travel, HR/ed-tech — not generic textbook filler.

---

## 📋 How to Use This Guide

This guide is a **complete reference manual** covering every major category of
non-functional testing. Parts are ordered for progressive learning, but each is
self-contained enough to jump to directly if you already know your gap.

**For interview prep**: every part ends with a "Common Interview Questions" section.
**Security-focused study**: Parts 4–5 get two full modules — deliberately the deepest
coverage in this course.
**Daily reference**: Part 12 (Test Planning) and Part 8 (Compatibility) are the ones
you'll come back to most on a real project.
